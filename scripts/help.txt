.tag Turn your computer off and on, type tmux, and it's all back. Your AI agents too.

= Get started
- Pick a pane by its number or the arrow keys. J jumps you there.
- Agent asking you something? Press Tab and answer it from here.
- S saves every pane, right now.

= Two AI sessions talking
1. Name one of them. In Claude Code, type `! agent-say name shop-dev`, or select its pane here and press N.
2. In the other one, just ask: "Use agent-say to tell shop-dev the tests pass."

= Linking a server
1. Open another terminal window.
2. ssh into the server. It has to log in with a key.
3. Make sure tmux-revive is installed there:
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odysseyalive/tmux-revive/main/install.sh | sh
4. Start `tmux`.
5. Back in this panel, press L to link it.

Now you can detach tmux and close that window. The server stays linked.
